# Building CoworkEnv

## Practical Lessons for Training Cowork Agents with Limited Resources

**CoworkEnv Team · 20 October 2026**

[Read the PDF](CoworkEnv_Lessons.pdf) · [Download the PDF](https://github.com/zhongweixie/CoworkEnv-Lessons/raw/refs/heads/main/CoworkEnv_Lessons.pdf) · [CoworkEnv project](https://github.com/HKUST-KnowComp/CoworkEnv)

These companion notes bring together 11 practical lessons from building and training cowork agents, organized into three parts:

- **Make tasks and training work:** executable task contracts, grading, supervision, and interaction protocols.
- **Make experiments affordable:** small-scale validation, usable feedback, compute choices, and recoverable checkpoints.
- **Turn observations into research:** learning mechanisms, process-feedback calibration, and experimental decisions.

The 14-page PDF includes concrete examples, practical recommendations, and a short operating guide. It covers development experience and subsequent exploration alongside the configurations and results documented in the CoworkEnv paper.
